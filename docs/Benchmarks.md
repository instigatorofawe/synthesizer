# Benchmarks

Generated by `tools/Bench.py` at `a06aadb`. Change the tool, not this file.

Cortex-M4 counts come from `firmware/Bench.cpp` on QEMU's mps2-an386, built with arm-none-eabi-g++ (15:14.2.rel1-1) 14.2.1 20241119 at -O2, frame 32, 48000 Hz. They are exact and repeat, so a change in one is a change in the code. Host figures come from Google Benchmark on one core of Rans-14-M4-Pro-Macbook-Pro.local, and move from run to run.

Section 17 of `docs/Design.md` applies: host figures rank kernels on that host only and are not carried to Cortex-M. Megahertz here is a model, instructions at 1.5 cycles each, with no flash wait states, cache misses or DMA. Only a board can replace it.

## Engine

A Program with nothing to play costs 29.0 instructions a sample. One voice of a naive saw, the cheapest voice that makes a sound, costs 92.8, and four cost 208.7, so each voice after the first adds 38.6. Its kernels account for 10.0 of that, which leaves 28.7 on every voice whatever it plays: setting up its context, checking both channels for non-finite samples, and adding them to the bus.
