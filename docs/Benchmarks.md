# Benchmarks

Generated by `tools/Bench.py` at `6e86369`. Change the tool, not this file.

## Engine

A Program with nothing to play costs 29.1 instructions a sample. One voice of a naive saw, the cheapest voice that makes a sound, costs 87.7, and four cost 185.7, so each voice after the first adds 32.7. Its kernels account for 10.2 of that, which leaves 22.5 on every voice whatever it plays: setting up its context, checking its output for non-finite samples, and adding it to the bus.
